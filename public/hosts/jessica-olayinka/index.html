About Jessica Olayinka

Jessica Olayinka is the host of Soul Sessions — MOR Radio's gospel and spiritual healing show that airs Wednesdays from 20:00 to 22:00. With a background in ministry and a deep love for gospel music, Jessica creates a space where faith and music intersect to bring healing and inspiration.

Each episode features a mix of classic gospel hymns, contemporary worship, and spiritual reflections on topics such as hope, purpose, and divine healing. The show also includes listener prayers and testimonies of God's faithfulness.

About Peter Onalaja

Peter Onalaja is the host of Business Focus — MOR Radio's entrepreneurship and finance show that airs Mondays from 19:00 to 21:00. With experience as a business consultant and entrepreneur, Peter provides practical advice on building businesses, managing personal finances, and navigating the corporate landscape.

Each episode features discussions on startup funding, financial literacy, career advancement, and interviews with successful African entrepreneurs and business leaders. The show aims to empower listeners with the knowledge and tools to achieve financial independence.
